Refund Policy

Last updated 2026-10-12. These are digital goods and automated services. Please read before buying.

Short version

We refund only if the product is defective — i.e. it was not delivered, or what you received is corrupt or unusable. We do not offer refunds for change of mind on a digital product that was delivered and works, because you can evaluate it with the free tool and the public samples before buying.

Kits (instant downloads)

If your download fails, is corrupted, or you were charged but received no file, contact us within 14 days with your transaction reference and we'll re-deliver it or, if we can't, refund that purchase. Once a working file has been delivered, the sale is final.

The paid audit

The audit delivers a scorecard either way — specific findings, or a clean result (no tells matched). A clean result is a valid outcome you paid for — you're paying for the check and the scorecard, not for us to find problems — so it is not grounds for a refund. We refund an audit only if we failed to deliver the agreed scorecard, or delivered something outside the agreed scope. We don't refund because you disagree with honest findings or because the result was clean.

Crypto payments are final

On-chain payments (USDC on Base, etc.) are irreversible and non-refundable. Once broadcast they can't be reversed, and we can't reliably verify that a refund request comes from the original payer — so crypto purchases are final. Always verify the address and amount before sending. (Card payments, where offered, may be refunded per this policy.)

How to request

Email m3v@tuta.io with your transaction reference and what went wrong. We aim to respond within a few business days.
