Why you can't be cheated

Fail-closed on both sides.

The buyer pins the recipient, amount, token and chain and pays the pinned address — never a value the server hands it — so a tampered or misconfigured endpoint can't redirect a payment. The server reads Base directly (correct chain, canonical USDC, exact amount, confirmations, two independent RPCs, single-use tx) and marks a payment used only after a signed badge is delivered. No payment, no badge. No fake green.

Being straight about what this is. This first payment was a controlled test with our own funds, to prove the mechanism end to end — it is not a customer sale, and we won't pretend otherwise. What it demonstrates: the pay → verify → signed-badge loop works, autonomously, and every badge is independently checkable. That's the part that matters.

A badge attests that a deterministic check ran on your input and what it found. It is not a guarantee your agent is correct or hallucination-free. Use it as a signal.

Nothing here is a guarantee, certification, financial advice, or a promise of any outcome. On-chain transactions are irreversible; verify every address and amount yourself before sending funds.
