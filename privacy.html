Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-12. We collect as little as possible. We don't sell your data.

The free scanner uploads nothing

The web scanner runs entirely in your browser (client-side JavaScript). The transcript you paste is not sent to us and not stored by us. The command-line version runs locally on your machine. We never see that content.

What we do receive

  • If you buy a kit: the on-chain payment (a public blockchain transaction) and standard web-server logs (IP, timestamp, request) needed to deliver your download. For card payments (where offered), our payment provider processes your details under their own privacy policy — we do not receive or store your full card data.
  • If you request a paid audit: the material you choose to send us for analysis, and the contact channel you used. We process that material only to produce your scorecard, and we don't publish it or reuse it to train anything.
  • If you email us: your message and address, to reply.

How we use it

Only to provide and deliver the Services, prevent abuse, and meet legal/tax obligations. We do not sell personal data, and we don't run third-party advertising trackers on this site.

Retention & your rights

We keep purchase and audit records as long as needed for the service and for legal/accounting requirements, then delete them. You can ask us what we hold about you, or to delete it, at m3v@tuta.io — subject to records we must keep by law.

Third parties

Hosting (static site), a payment processor, and public blockchain infrastructure are involved in delivering the Services. On-chain transactions are public and permanent by design.
