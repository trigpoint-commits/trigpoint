The paid audit — scope & limits

The agent-integrity audit is an affordable, fast first-pass triage — it is NOT an enterprise red-team. Unless otherwise agreed in writing, each audit covers:

  • ONE agent, and up to a reasonable single-session transcript (guideline: ≤ ~1 MB / ~6,000 messages of text you provide);
  • a findings scorecard: integrity "tells" quoted from your material, ranked, with suggested fixes;
  • best-effort turnaround (typically within a few business days), delivered by automated analysis with light review;
  • $0 if we find no confirmed issues — if the triage surfaces nothing material, you owe nothing.

Out of scope: live/production access, compliance certification, penetration testing, legal sign-off, ongoing monitoring, or anything implying an enterprise-grade or regulator-grade assurance. We may decline or refund a request that is out of scope.
